Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Plain-language summary: PhotoGlow processes photos on your device. We do not receive your photos, do not operate an image-processing server, do not require an account, and do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoGlow ("PhotoGlow," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the PhotoGlow iOS application and this support website.

2. Photos and image processing

When you choose a photo from Photos or Files, PhotoGlow decodes, analyzes, enhances, compresses, or converts that photo locally on your device using Apple system frameworks and bundled Core ML models.

We do not upload, receive, or collect:

  • Your photos, enhanced outputs, or thumbnails.
  • Image metadata, including EXIF or location metadata.
  • Image filenames or local file paths.
  • Your image-processing history or enhancement settings as remote analytics.

Location metadata is removed from exports by default. If you enable the option to preserve location, PhotoGlow includes the source location metadata in the exported file you create. That file is saved or shared only through the destination you select.

3. Information stored on your device

PhotoGlow stores the following information locally so the app can function:

  • App preferences, such as the default export format and whether to preserve location metadata.
  • A daily free-export allowance record stored in the iOS Keychain. This record helps prevent a device clock change or reinstall from creating extra free exports and may remain after the app is deleted.
  • Temporary source, intermediate, and exported image files in the app's sandbox while a task is active or until cleanup occurs.

You can remove temporary files from PhotoGlow Settings. iOS removes the app sandbox when you delete the app. Keychain data is controlled by iOS and may persist until it is removed by the operating system or the device is erased.

4. Photos and Files access

PhotoGlow uses Apple's photo picker and file picker so you can choose input images. PhotoGlow requests add-only Photos permission when you ask it to save an output to your photo library. You can review or change Photos access in iOS Settings. PhotoGlow uses selected files only to perform the action you request.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. PhotoGlow uses StoreKit to display products, start purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether PhotoGlow Pro is active. We do not receive or store your full payment card details or Apple Account password.

Apple handles purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may provide us with transaction status and aggregated sales information needed to operate subscriptions and understand App Store performance.

6. Diagnostics

PhotoGlow does not include a third-party crash reporting or analytics SDK. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers through your Apple device settings, Apple may provide us with diagnostic information such as crash reports and device or operating-system characteristics. Apple controls that process under its privacy terms. We do not intentionally place photo content, EXIF metadata, or full filenames in diagnostic logs.

7. This website and support email

This is a static GitHub Pages website. It contains no account system, contact form, advertising, tracking pixel, or analytics script. GitHub may process basic network and security information when it serves the pages, such as an IP address and browser request, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use that information only to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and maintain an appropriate support record. Please do not send private photos unless you decide they are necessary. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence, subject to legal and security retention needs.

8. Sale, sharing, and tracking

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. We do not share photo content with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics providers. Information may be disclosed only when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to the service providers described above when necessary to provide App Store purchases, website hosting, or email support.

9. Children

PhotoGlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

10. Your choices

  • Choose which photos or files PhotoGlow can access.
  • Disable or change Photos permission in iOS Settings.
  • Remove temporary files from PhotoGlow Settings.
  • Delete PhotoGlow to remove its app sandbox and preferences.
  • Manage diagnostic sharing in iOS privacy settings.
  • Contact us about support correspondence or privacy questions.

Because we do not operate an account or image-processing server, we generally do not hold an app-user profile or photo library that we can retrieve, export, or delete.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PhotoGlow's features or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how the app handles information, we will provide additional notice where required.

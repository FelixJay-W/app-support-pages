Requirements and formats

  • PhotoGlow requires iOS 16 or later and is designed for iPhone.
  • Supported still-image formats are JPEG, PNG, and HEIC.
  • RAW, animated GIF, PSD, and video files are not supported in version 1.0.
  • Enhancement performance depends on image size, available storage, memory, and device temperature.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photo analysis and enhancement run on your iPhone using models bundled with PhotoGlow. PhotoGlow does not upload your photos, thumbnails, filenames, or image metadata to developer-operated servers.

How does the free allowance work?

Free users can successfully export up to three AI-enhanced photos per local calendar day. Previewing an enhancement or encountering a processing failure does not use a free export. Compression and format conversion do not use the AI allowance.

What does PhotoGlow Pro include?

PhotoGlow Pro unlocks unlimited AI enhancements, full-resolution and 2x/4x export options, and sequential batch processing for up to 20 photos. Actual processing speed and continuous use depend on device performance, memory, and temperature.

How do I restore a subscription?

In PhotoGlow, open Settings and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. PhotoGlow does not use a separate account.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, select your name, then select Subscriptions. You can also visit Apple's subscription management page.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Why did enhancement pause or fail?

Large images can require significant memory and storage. Close other demanding apps, free some device storage, allow a warm iPhone to cool, and try again. A failed enhancement or save does not use a free export.

How do I remove local PhotoGlow data?

Use Settings > Clear Temporary Files inside PhotoGlow to remove temporary images. Deleting PhotoGlow removes its app sandbox. A small daily allowance record stored securely in the iOS Keychain may remain after reinstalling, as permitted by iOS.
